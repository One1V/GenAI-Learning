## What is fine-tuning ?

**The purpose of fine-tuning is to adapt an existing model to a particular task, domain, behaviour or response format by training it further on a dataset**

**Example: Fine-tuning Llama 2 on banking data**

Suppose we have a pre-trained Llama 2 model and a dataset containing 10,000 banking-related question-answer pairs.

**Step 1: Existing model**

Llama 2 already understands general language, reasoning patterns and various subjects.

**Step 2: Provide our dataset**

We give it examples such as:


| Question                   | Expected answer         |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| What is an FD?             | Fixed Deposit is...     |
| What is a savings account? | A savings account is... |
| What is compound interest? | Compound interest is... |

**Step 3: Fine-tuning**

The model learns from these examples by adjusting its weights to better reproduce the desired responses.

**Step 4: Future questions**

Now suppose someone asks:

"What happens when I prematurely withdraw my FD?"

The fine-tuned model can use its existing knowledge and learned banking-related patterns to generate an appropriate answer.

### **One important distinction**

Fine-tuning does not mean the model memorizes the entire dataset or guarantees that every answer will come directly from it.

It learns patterns, task-specific behaviour and relationships from training examples.

Also, if you want a model to answer questions using a frequently changing private knowledge base, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) may be more appropriate than fine-tuning alone.

### **Remember this distinction**


| Fine-tuning                              | RAG                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Changes model weights                    | Retrieves external information                                 |
| Learns task-specific patterns            | Supplies relevant context                                      |
| Requires additional training             | Usually doesn't require model retraining when documents change |
| Useful for adapting behaviour and output | Useful for answering from specific knowledge sources           |

**Interview question to test your understanding:**

Suppose I have a general Llama 2 model and 5,000 company policy documents. I want employees to ask questions and receive answers based on the latest company policies.

Would you choose fine-tuning or RAG, and why?
